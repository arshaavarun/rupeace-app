RuPeace privacy policy

Effective 28 November 2026 · applies to the RuPeace Android app, version 2.0 and later

In short: what you type into RuPeace is kept in a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive and on your phone. RuPeace has no server of its own: the developer cannot see, read or sell your data. There are no ads and no tracking.

What RuPeace keeps, and where

  • Your household's money records: entries (income, spends, savings, loan payments, transfers), accounts and cards, categories and budgets, loans, savings places, recurring items, events, bombs, and the names (and, if you add them, email addresses) of family members. These are stored in a Google Sheet called "RuPeace · your family name" that the app creates in the Google Drive of the person who starts the family, and in a copy on each family phone so the app works offline.
  • Backups: copies of the same records as files in a "RuPeace backups" folder in your Google Drive, made daily by the app and when you ask.
  • Your PIN: kept only on your phone, as a salted hash (never the PIN itself). Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; RuPeace never receives your fingerprint or face data.

Your Google account

You sign in with Google. RuPeace uses:

  • your name, email address and profile photo, to show who is signed in and to let you invite family members by their Gmail address;
  • Google Drive (only files RuPeace creates) (drive.file), to create the family Sheet and the backups folder and to find them again;
  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets), to read and write the family Sheet, including a family Sheet that a family member has shared with you.

RuPeace's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide the app's features to you, is never transferred to anyone else (except the family members you choose to share the family Sheet with), never used for advertising, and never read by people.

Sharing

When you invite someone, RuPeace shares the family Sheet with their Google account from your Drive, so they can see and change the family's records. Nothing is shared with the developer or with any other company. RuPeace does not sell data.

What leaves your phone

  • Your records go only to Google (the Drive and Sheets APIs), over an encrypted connection, to your family Sheet and backups.
  • The app's screens are loaded from GitHub Pages (github.io). Like any website, GitHub may log your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement. No records are sent to GitHub.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising services are used.

Deleting your data

In the app: tap your photo (top right) → Delete my RuPeace data. If you started the family, its Sheet goes to the bin of your Google Drive (and, if you tick it, your backups too); if you joined a family, you leave it. RuPeace is then signed out and its access to your Google account is removed.

Without the app, see how to delete your RuPeace data. Because RuPeace has no server, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

RuPeace is meant for adults managing a household's money. It is not directed at children under 13.
