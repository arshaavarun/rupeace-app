Delete your RuPeace data

RuPeace for Android

RuPeace has no server and no RuPeace account of its own: you sign in with Google, and your household's records live only in your own Google Drive and on your phone. Deleting them is in your hands, and it is complete: nothing is kept anywhere else.

In the app

  1. Open RuPeace and tap your photo at the top right.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete my RuPeace data.
  3. Tick Also delete my backups if you want them gone too, type DELETE, and tap Delete.

If you started the family, its Google Sheet goes to the bin of your Google Drive, so everyone in the family loses it. If you joined a family, you leave it and its records stay with its owner. RuPeace is then signed out and its access to your Google account is removed.

Without the app

  1. In Google Drive, delete the Sheet named RuPeace · your family name and the folder RuPeace backups, then empty the bin (or wait: Google deletes things in the bin for good after 30 days).
  2. Remove RuPeace's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  3. Uninstall the app to remove the copy on your phone, including your PIN.

What is deleted

All of it: entries, accounts, categories, loans, savings, recurring items, events, bombs, family members, backups, and the PIN on your phone. Nothing is retained by the developer, because nothing was ever sent to the developer.
